DATE-03 and DATE-07 for the reports, finance-report and dashboard family: switch the period
predicates AND the call sites that feed them, in one pass, so no two surfaces disagree about the
same week — and prove that a fixed past period's totals are byte-identical across the migration.

`reports.py`'s predicate to compare a date-only column while `routes/reports.py` still passes
UTC-timestamp bounds is exactly the Pitfall-14 bug: it happens to work at `Europe/Moscow` and
silently drops rows at UTC and every negative offset.

(`stale_products` explicitly NOT switched).
Output: three service modules and three route modules switched, plus VA-9.
</objective>

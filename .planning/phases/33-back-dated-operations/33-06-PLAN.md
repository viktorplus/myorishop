<locked_planner_decision>
**The period-bounds contract is CLOSED `[start_day, end_day]`, inclusive on both ends.**

`local_day_bounds_utc` is half-open `[start, end)` because a row landing exactly on UTC midnight
would otherwise double-count; a date-string comparison has no such hazard, and closed removes the
`+1 day` arithmetic entirely. Consequence, and the one thing that must be applied MECHANICALLY:
every switched predicate turns `created_at >= start_iso` / `created_at < end_iso` into
`business_date_expr(M) >= start_day` / `business_date_expr(M) <= end_day` — `<` becomes `<=`. Nine
predicates switch in one pass and exactly one of them keeping `<` is Pitfall D, a silent one-day
off-by-one across nine reports. State the contract in the docstring FIRST, then apply it.
</locked_planner_decision>
